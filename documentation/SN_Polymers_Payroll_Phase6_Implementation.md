## Architecture and formulas

The existing authoritative source RPC, rational-arithmetic engine, per-employee review transitions, reporting and Accounts beneficiary tables remain the foundation. No client-supplied payable values, parallel salary calculator or mutable balance ledger is introduced.

- Permanent entitlement comes from effective salary structure and policy. Joining/leaving dates are inclusive; published policy selects monthly gross × employed calendar days ÷ actual month days or the configured fixed divisor (30 for the approved Fixed30 option). Effective salary changes within a month still require a separately approved allocation rule.
- One monthly entitlement is allocated by covered employment calendar days, with the final chronological run receiving the approved rounding residual. Frozen monthly plans and prior base/LOP allocations must match later calculations. Complete monthly coverage is required. Split allocations containing sub-paise base/LOP amounts block finalization pending an approved net residual rule; per-run paise rounding must not silently inflate the month total.
- LOP uses the configured Fixed30/Actual Days divisor and approved DAILY or MONTH/AGGREGATE rounding. LINE remains blocked until a line definition is approved. Published paid non-working calendar days are excluded even inside an unpaid leave range; absence of calendar evidence is a blocker. The zero-pay exception requires explicit unpaid evidence for all employment days and the published no-payable-entitlement policy. Paid rest entitlement cannot disappear through a cap.
- Monthly Special Packages contribute one contractual entitlement; component diagnostics reconcile the agreed gross and are not additional earnings. Existing factory wage, holiday, double-duty and overtime calculations are reused. Unsupported factory exceptions remain blocked.
- EPF/ESI/PT resolve verified, effective-dated employee profiles and published canonical-scope masters. Explicit NOT_APPLICABLE yields zero; unknown applicability yields no deduction and a blocker. Contributions use combined eligible month earnings and configured rates/ceilings or half-open PT slabs, then configured rounding. Remaining collection equals recomputed monthly liability minus prior immutable collections. Employer contributions remain separate. Missing eligibility, wage component inclusion, LOP treatment, timing or required arrears treatment blocks. No rates/slabs are hardcoded.
- Approved bonus/arrears are typed earnings. Statutory inclusion is explicit. Accounts records an already externally issued advance; independent HO approval authorizes its evidenced installment/start plan. Recovery = minimum(outstanding principal, approved monthly installment less earlier same-month collections). Previewing never reduces the balance. Plan revisions require renewed approval; pause/resume requires HO. No interest, ordinary loan policy or inferred repayment is introduced.
- Final net = exact earnings − LOP − applicable employee statutory deductions − approved recoveries, then HALF_UP to two decimals. Negative net blocks; valid zero net needs no payment. Scheme rounding applies at its own configured stage.

## Persistence, concurrency and permissions

Forward migrations 116–130 extend the earlier uncommitted 114 profile foundation and 115 beneficiary link. Published statutory end dates can remain open; supersession closes applicability while preserving original publication history. Published calendar classifications enforce consistent paid holiday/unpaid closure evidence.

Accounts/Admin manage statutory setup and confirmed profiles; Admin retains payroll policy administration. Accounts proposes a closed-period cutoff, independently approved by HO. Existing factory grants protect Factory Manager ledger/review scopes; no HO/Project salary access is inferred. Bonus/arrears preparers cannot self-approve. Advance preparation is Accounts/Admin, final approval is independent HO.

The existing `approve_lock` transition calls a private transaction finalizer only when the owner-only financial gate permits it. It serializes employee/month work, protects source revisions during revalidation, checks row version/fingerprint, reviewer authorization and required prior review, cutoff, complete calculation/net, statutory history and ledger allowances. Atomic writes create immutable financial snapshots, exclusive employee-date coverage, monthly entitlement/base/LOP allocations, statutory entries and adjustment applications. A failure rolls back the whole transaction. Locked rows, financial snapshots and history cannot be edited; adjustments apply once. Migrations 127–128 replace the original source-table SHARE locks with transaction-scoped advisory fences. Employee sources use employee identity; attendance headers use sheet identity; calendar days use group/department/month; policy and factory rules use group/category. Statutory master configuration uses a separate shared fence. Header/run fences are bounded to relevant source months and preceding-day compensatory-off evidence. Source-write triggers cover inserts, updates and deletes, including both OLD/NEW identities. Finalization shares the relevant fences; source writers acquire exclusive fences (attendance row writers share their header fence). Operational source conflicts reject with a retryable 409 instead of waiting after operational row locks. New immutable factory revision inserts retain their original category serialization, preserving successful concurrent distinct-date creation; finalization fails fast against an active writer. Unrelated attendance/leave writes can proceed during finalization. Employee/month financial serialization, fingerprint revalidation and the activation gate remain intact.

`payroll_financial_enabled()` remains false and is inaccessible to ordinary/service callers. Tests temporarily substitute gate/clock as database owner solely in the isolated project and restore them. There is no API/UI activation switch. Existing posting gates continue to prohibit bank/payment/statutory liability posting.

## Outstanding dependencies and deliberate deferrals

Production requires published effective policies, complete Accounts statutory configuration, verified employee enrolment/applicability and canonical mappings, calendar evidence, approved cutoff/ledger plans, UAT and explicit financial activation approval. Bank details alone are not payment verification.

Unresolved scenarios remain blocked: salary changes within a month, unsupported factory exceptions/short-hour rules, statutory scheme exemptions/minimums/cap-share semantics without executable configuration, DAILY/LINE statutory rounding, negative statutory residual adjustments, original-month statutory arrears, and sub-paise split-net reconciliation. Published calendar replacement/correction and post-lock reversal/recovery transactions beyond supported positive linked arrears are deferred rather than mutating history. No legacy Admin draft ownership handoff is invented.

Payments, disbursements, bank integrations, external advance repayments, statutory settlement/posting and accounting settlement remain excluded. Payment status is informational NOT_RECORDED (or NOT_REQUIRED for valid zero net), not a payment ledger. Scope is a guarded, tested financial salary workflow, not unrestricted production readiness for every policy scenario.
