## Follow-up clarification and leave research — 12 October 2026

The user confirmed that “gross by 39” was a typo: **offer Fixed30 and Actual Calendar Days**. Neither option silently changes LOP, and selecting a method in a draft is not retroactive approval for an existing published revision. HO finally approves bonuses, arrears and advance recoveries; evidence requirements and recovery limits remain unresolved. A subsequent explicit reply confirms Accounts owns statutory master setup; the earlier Admin-only setup decision is superseded. This is a client role/workflow decision, not permission to invent missing statutory parameters or mark unverified employee facts as verified.

For weekly offs/holidays inside an unpaid leave range, the user asked for online research and delegated selection of a suitable convention. There is no verified majority-company rule in the sources reviewed. [Zoho People’s own configuration documentation](https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/people/administrator-guide/leave/settings/articles/additonal-options) offers calendar-day/business-day and inclusion/exclusion choices; this shows policy variability, not which policy most employers use.

[Code on Wages, 2019, section 20](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/15793/1/aA2019-29.pdf) ties absence deductions to time the employee was required to work and limits their proportion relative to that required work. This supports examining scheduled duties and entitlement instead of treating every date in a request as an unpaid absence. It does not by itself settle every weekly-rest, holiday, worker-category or state-specific entitlement, or authorize the payroll divisor.

**Research-selected business rule:** exclude normally paid, non-working weekly offs/holidays from LOP, even when explicitly inside a single unpaid-leave date range. Count only evidenced chargeable working days. This preserves the already approved no-sandwich policy. If Friday and Monday are unpaid working days and Saturday/Sunday are paid non-working days, a Friday–Monday request contributes **two** LOP days. A regular Saturday working day would instead be chargeable. Do not infer all Sundays are paid or apply permanent monthly entitlement automatically to daily-wage employees.

Applicable work calendars, holiday/rest entitlement and finalized leave evidence must determine the classification. If those facts are missing/conflicting, keep calculation blocked rather than guessing. The zero-pay full-month exception applies only when **no payable entitlement exists**, not merely when all working days were unpaid; retained paid rest/holiday entitlement cannot be discarded through a zero cap. The existing calculator’s unresolved weekly-off blocker is not removed by this documentation update. Advisor confirmation remains required for the applicable legal treatment.

This follow-up changes the decision record only; no new calculation formula, role permission, financial lock or payment workflow is activated.
